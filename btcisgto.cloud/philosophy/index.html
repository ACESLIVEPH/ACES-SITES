MY WORKING PHILOSOPHY

Life is a game.
Play with purpose.

Making money is a game. Learning, creating and building relationships all involve choices, incentives and uncertainty. The useful question is: what kind of game am I playing, and what does a good outcome mean?

For relationships, the frame is cooperation: mutual happiness, trust and shared experiences. The person across from you is not an opponent to defeat.

“GTO” is the guiding idea behind this site. Applying game theory to life is a philosophy to examine, not proof of a mathematically optimal life strategy.

THE PLAYBOOK / QUESTIONS TO TEST

Before the next move.

UNDERSTAND

What are the rules?

Identify the incentives, costs and constraints. Separate what you know from what you assume.

DECIDE

What can I lose?

Choose the risk before the outcome. One lucky result does not establish a repeatable strategy.

COOPERATE

Can we both win?

Look for shared goals, clear communication and relationships worth continuing.

REFLECT

Was it worth playing?

Review the money and the experience. Keep the lesson, including the uncomfortable ones.
