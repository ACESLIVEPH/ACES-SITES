How this practice model works

Equity = available points + reserved pending margin and fee + estimated exit value of open positions. Net trade P&L includes both fees and slippage. Margin is isolated: a position cannot consume more than its margin plus entry fee. This simplified loss cap is not how every exchange liquidates positions.

Pending orders reserve points. Limit fills respect their limit; stop entries execute at the next observed price with slippage. Stops and targets are checked only on received ticks, never retrospectively against chart candles. Gaps can exceed a planned stop. Live quotes older than 15 seconds cannot execute orders. The feed is polled, not tick-perfect.

Practice mode uses seed 42 and coherent cross rates. Changing source starts a new session. Sessions save only in this browser; no orders execute while the page is closed. The journal export includes rules, quote times and source. Local records can be edited and cannot support paid competitions or verified performance claims.

Price provenance: Binance public market-data API. Availability depends on network and region. Synthetic prices are explicitly fictional. No API key, exchange account or wallet is required.
