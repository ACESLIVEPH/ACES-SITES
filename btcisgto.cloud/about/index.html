Jay Lee.
A mission with roots.

Builder, writer, and Bitcoiner. The person behind BTCisGTO and the long road toward Lola’s HOME.

From a comfortable life to a larger purpose

I was born in Angeles City and grew up across five countries. For a long time, I struggled with belonging and with understanding who I was. I studied Psychology and Criminology at Florida State University, trying to understand minds and the systems we live in.

I had much of what the world said I should want—family, health, money, and stability. Yet I was privately unhappy. Seeing people search through trash for food broke through that comfortable bubble.

“Heal yourself first. Then help heal others.”

A deeply personal experience on a mountain in Colombia changed how I understood my name and my purpose. I came to see “Healer” as a calling: work on myself, then build something that can serve other people.

Bitcoin. Truth. Compassion.

Bitcoin became part of my strategy for long-term sovereignty. Truth means showing the work and checking the numbers. Compassion is the reason to do any of it.

BTCisGTO brings those ideas together in a public body of work: the writing, the tools, the lessons, and the mistakes. ACES is the platform vision. Lola’s HOME is the destination.

Follow the work, not just the story

The mission still has a long way to go. The best way to understand it is to try what exists, ask questions, and watch what gets delivered next.
