How will the numbers be verified?

Monthly reports will reconcile opening assets, contributions, receipts, costs, trading results, liabilities and closing assets. Internal transfers won’t count as new income. Paper results will stay separate from live performance.

On-chain transfers can be checked publicly. Business records explain what those transfers mean. Corrections and losses belong in the record too. Customer information, private keys and sensitive live orders stay private.
