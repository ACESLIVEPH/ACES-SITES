The operating charter

Start with a $1,000 trading bankroll. At $1,200 of settled balance after trading costs, allocate $100 to BTC and retain $1,100. At $1,320, allocate $110 and retain $1,210. Overshoots split the actual qualifying profit equally. No tax provision is deducted by this rule; tax obligations remain separate.

Losses do not reset the reference downward. New deposits are not profit. Close all positions and cancel pending orders before a banking checkpoint. Conversion and transfer costs come from the BTC allocation. Only confirmed native BTC received at the mission address counts as banked.

Evidence before authority

Edge stores the hypothesis. The Trading Sim rehearses execution. Hydra tests fixed rules on held-out data. The shared workspace links the brief and paper reports for review. Historical success qualifies only for forward paper research. Venue eligibility, realistic execution, independent reconciliation and owner authorization are still required before any live pilot.

The current simulator uses simplified costs and does not model venue-specific prediction contracts, order-book depth or perpetual funding. Its 10,000 PTS sessions are normalized to a $1,000 equivalent only for mission-policy illustrations. Each experiment is independent; paper allocations never increase the public BTC reserve.

Keep the vault separate

Agents have no withdrawal authority or cold-wallet keys. Banked BTC never returns to trading. The public treasury observes the published address; ownership, source attribution and trading profitability require supporting records. A local research brief or report is editable and is never labeled verified.
